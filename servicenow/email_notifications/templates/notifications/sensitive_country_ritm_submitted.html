Thank you for notifying us of your planned travel to ${current.variables.destination} from ${current.variables.travel_start_date} to ${current.variables.travel_end_date}.

Since ${current.variables.destination} is classified as a sensitive country for compliance purposes, the following restrictions will apply on the travel start date:

  • You will be automatically added to the EC-Restricted-China-Travel AD group and ZTNA_vpn-restricted-chinatravel Entra ID group for the duration of your travel. 

What's restricted?

Certain chip design content, specifically PHY design data and classified as 3E001 in Ampere, Hopper, and nvgpu mainline, is restricted in China and Hong Kong

Most of your daily tools (Teams, VPN, GitLab, Slack, etc.) will not be impacted. Impact depends on your specific role:

  • Non-Chip Design: Zero impact to your access.
  • Chip Design: Minimal impact. If you find a Perforce path blocked, please reach out to EngSec-Support@nvidia.com.
  • PHY Design: Access to PHY data (3E001/Ampere/Hopper) will be restricted while you are in the region.
