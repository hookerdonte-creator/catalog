Your new computer is being prepared

Good News . Your new computer is being prepared. You will receive another notification when your computer is ready to be picked up.

Here’s what you need to do:

Prior to receiving your new computer

Backup data on your current computer

  • Review the "OneDrive for Business Instructions – Refresh" document for detailed instructions, which explains how to use OneDrive to backup data from your current computer and access it on your new computer.
  • Depending on volume and internet speed, migrating your data can take 3-4 hours

Receiving your new computer

Onsite Pickup

  • Since you have selected “pickup” as your preferred delivery option, please visit your selected Tech Lounge/Office to pick up your computer.
  • Ensure your data is backed up to OneDrive for business prior to setting up your new computer.
  • Be ready to return your current IT equipment once your new computer is set up. A return label will be provided, or you can drop off your old computer at any tech lounge.

Return your new computer

  • Please drop off your computer at the nearest office IT tech lounge.

Setting up your new computer

Follow the Windows or Mac Setup Instructions document to provision your new computer.
