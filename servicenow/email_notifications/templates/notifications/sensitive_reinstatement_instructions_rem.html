Access Reinstatement

Once you have submitted the reinstatement attestation, please allow 2 hours for the change to be reflected in downstream systems like Perforce, Confluence, etc.

If you are still having access issues after 2 hours:

  1. Confluence - Log out and log back in to refresh your session/permissions.
  2. Linux - Open a new shell or run 'newgrp <unixgroup>' to refresh your permissions (In all open terminals).
  3. Nvidia Laptop/Network - You should regain access to standard network resources and default gateways.

GlobalProtect/VPN Connection

After the 2-hour window following attestation, you should be re-enabled to access non-China gateways and be able to connect to the standard gateway(s) on your primary laptop.
