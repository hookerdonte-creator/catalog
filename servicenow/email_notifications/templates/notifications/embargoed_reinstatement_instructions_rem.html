To avoid delays and disruptions upon your return to work, follow the instructions below to regain NVIDIA account access after you have returned from travel. Your manager is responsible for requesting the reinstatement of your access upon your return.

Employee to Notify Your Manager

  • Upon your return, inform your manager so they can initiate the reinstatement of your access.

Manager's Required Actions for Direct Report's Access Reinstatement:

  • Log into your NVIDIA Help account
  • Navigate to My Requests
  • Find your travel ticket
    • Labeled as Embargoed/Sensitive Country Travel
  • Click on Actions
  • Click on Reinstate Access
  • Submit the Reinstatement Attestation

Employee's Access Reinstatement:

  • Once your direct manager submits the Reinstatement Attestation, please allow 1 hour for the system to update, then log in and check your credentials.
  • Please note that due to account disablement, you will be removed from both public and private Slack channels. You will need to request re-access to any necessary channels once your account is restored. 
  • The reinstatement of the DL groups you were previously part of will happen automatically and may take a few days to fully take effect after your account is restored. 

For any issues, please contact the Deemed Export team at nvidia-deemedexport@nvidia.com.
